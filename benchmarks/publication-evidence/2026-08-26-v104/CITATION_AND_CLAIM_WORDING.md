# Citation / claim wording draft

Until a broader systematic novelty review is complete, prefer:

> We report an exhaustive finite-order computation on the complete catalog of
> 12,005,168 non-isomorphic simple graphs with 10 vertices. Restricting each
> vertex-deleted card to the aligned atomic family
> `{e1,...,e9,tree}`, we determine the exact minimum atomic-field counts for
> auxiliary bit budgets 0 through 6 as `[5,3,3,2,2,2,1]`, with matching lower
> and upper certificates.

Do **not** use:

- "first complete graph invariant for n=10";
- "world first";
- "solves graph isomorphism";
- "solves the Reconstruction Conjecture";
- "optimal graph invariant" without the finite-n=10/fixed-family qualifier.
